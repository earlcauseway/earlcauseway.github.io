A community campaign to keep Earlswood Causeway open and protect direct access to residents' homes and the local amenity that matters to residents.

Support us

What we're doing

We are challenging Warwickshire County Council's decision for a trial 18-month closure of Earlswood Lakes Causeway.

The majority of Malthouse Lane residents oppose the closure.

The residents' campaign is calling for Earlswood Causeway to remain open.

Why it matters

The causeway plays a vital role in connecting our community, local businesses and access to residents' homes. It is also the most direct route for emergency services access.

For example.. with the causeway closed, the journey to the village post office from Malthouse Lane will be, on average, 3 times longer (with increased costs for fuel, wear and tear).

Similarly, longer distances will be required for GP appointments and healthcare visits along with many other public amenities.
